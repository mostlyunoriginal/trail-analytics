## Shared capture checklist

- Confirm access and the candidate's actual location.
- Slow overlapping orbits at waist/head/overhead height; capture each intended driving line.
- Include a measured scale reference; lock exposure; enable location tagging.
- Record observation date, direction, conditions, and capture filenames.

A nearby video is context. Coverage requires an explicitly verified target and a timestamp (or photo).
